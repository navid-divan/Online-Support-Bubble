Built around five guarantees

The same privacy properties proven in the paper, made tangible in an interactive interface.

🎯

Attribute-based selection

Choose advisors by what they can do, “cancer specialist”, “legal advisor”, “10+ years”, never by who they are.

🔗

Unlinkability

Every membership uses a freshly derived key. No one can link you across two bubbles, or tell whether you are in any bubble at all.

🛡️

Non-frameability

Messages are bound to derived keys, so nobody, not even the moderators, can impersonate you or frame you.

⚖️

Accountable tracing

Misbehaviour can be traced to a real identity, but only when the Registrar and the Tracer cooperate. Neither can do it alone.

✉️

Encrypted group messaging

Sign-then-encrypt broadcast keeps conversations confidential to the bubble and authenticated to each member.

🗄️

Server-backed

This edition replaces the paper’s distributed ledger with a single server behind a clean interface, the same protocol, simpler to run.

How a bubble comes together

Four steps, mirrored exactly by the dashboards.

1

Register

Subjects and advisors enrol once; advisors declare their attributes.

2

Form by policy

A subject states a policy, a set of requirements, and the system finds matching, available advisors.

3

Advisors join

Each invited advisor joins under a fresh, unlinkable key and the group is live.

4

Message & moderate

Members exchange encrypted messages; the subject can replace, remove or close at any time.

A dashboard for every role

Switch instantly between all five actors to see the protocol from every side.

🙋

Subject

Build a bubble by policy, message advisors, replace or remove them, and close the group.

🩺

Advisor

Review invitations, toggle availability, join bubbles and advise.

🗂️

Registrar

Holds the identity register and judges a revocation token back to a real person.

🔎

Tracer

Traces a misbehaving member to an anonymous revocation token.

▤

Ledger / Server

The append-only store, only ever sees unlinkable keys, never names.

⏱️

Profiler

Its own time & space logs, separate from the terminal build, downloadable any time.
